SOURCE TRANSPARENCY

About this digital archive

This archive provides public access to the scanned publication titled Is Anambra State Cursed or Are We the Cause?, which describes projects, policy measures and public-service interventions associated with Peter Obi's administration in Anambra State.

All photographs and primary captions are preserved within the original scanned pages. The thematic summaries are a modern editorial guide to the source and should not be mistaken for independently verified achievements, public spending audits or present-day assessments.

The NDC identity brands this contemporary digital presentation. It does not imply that NDC originally published the historical material, nor does this archive establish any current party nomination or endorsement.

Text search and text reading rely on automated optical character recognition. Verify all names, figures and quotations against the image of the original page, especially where scans are blurred.
